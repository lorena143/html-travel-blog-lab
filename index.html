Adventures in Fire Island

Visiting Fire Island National Seashore offered a peaceful coastal escape just a short ferry ride from the bustle of Long Island. Walking along the car-free boardwalks revealed sweeping Atlantic beaches, rolling sand dunes, and the rare maritime holly trees of the Sunken Forest. Spotting white-tailed deer along the trails and taking in panoramic views from the historic Fire Island Lighthouse made the trip both relaxing and memorable.

Explore the Adventure!

If you are looking for a refreshing getaway filled with natural beauty and outdoor adventure, Fire Island is well worth the visit. Whether you want to sunbathe on quiet shores, explore scenic trails, or enjoy charming beachside communities, this barrier island offers something for every traveler.
